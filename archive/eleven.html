Why 11:11?

There's honestly not much to explain here.

It's just something that slowly became a habit.

I know this might seem a little cheesy or even cringey, and maybe you'll laugh at it. That's completely okay.

Whenever I happened to notice the time was 11:11, I'd quietly think about you and wish that good things happened to you.

It wasn't something I planned or forced—it just became one of those small things I naturally did.

A while ago I had the idea of taking a screenshot whenever I caught the clock at 11:11, so I started saving those moments.

That's all this page is.

Nothing complicated.

Just a collection of little moments that reminded me of you.
