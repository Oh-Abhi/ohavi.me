Before Anything Else

First of all, thank you. Thank you for taking the time to read through all of this and for making it till the end.

I know life gets busy, and you didn't have to spend your time exploring this website, but the fact that you did means more to me than I can put into words.

Every page before this—the songs, the playlists, the 11:11 screenshots—had a specific reason to exist. None of it was added just to fill space. This entire website is simply my way of appreciating you and celebrating having you in my life.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for reading. Thank you for being exactly who you are.

I'm Sorry

Before saying anything else, I owe you a genuine apology.

Looking back, I realize there were times when I complained too much, held too many expectations, and made things far more chaotic than they ever needed to be.

I want to acknowledge my mistakes honestly, without making excuses or shifting blame onto you. I am truly sorry for any moment I made you feel overwhelmed or unheard.

The Things I Never Said

There are so many quiet details about you that I've noticed over time—things you probably don't even realize people notice.

I've noticed your incredible patience, the quiet kindness in how you treat people, and the small ways you make everything around you feel a little brighter.

You have this subtle warmth that makes people feel safe and understood, even when you aren't trying to.

Those little things are the exact reason why you became so genuinely important to me.

Behind This Project

I've been thinking about building something like this for a very long time. What started as a tiny draft slowly turned into a complete passion project.

If you wondered why I quietly disappeared for a while—this was the reason. I spent countless hours tweaking design details, refining playlists, writing synced lyrics, and assembling memories.

There were so many pages that never made it—ideas I scrapped because they didn't feel right. This isn't even the full vision I originally had, but I decided to share it now rather than making you wait for perfection.

Looking Ahead

This website isn't meant to be a static project that stays frozen in time. It's something that will keep growing.

I'll continue updating the songs whenever I discover new tracks that remind me of you. There are also new ideas planned—private posts, organized saved collections, and more quiet little corners.

Think of this as a quiet room that will always be here for you whenever you feel like revisiting it.

Goodbye (For This Website)

And so, we've arrived at the end of this letter.

Thank you again for being here, for reading, and for sharing this experience with me. I genuinely wish you all the happiness, warmth, and peace in the world.

Whether you spent five minutes here today or revisit this years from now, I'm simply glad you experienced something built with genuine care.

Take good care of yourself. Always. ♥
